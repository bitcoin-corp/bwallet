What we keep

When you talk to b without your own AI key, each message you send, b's reply and when it happened are kept, linked to your account. Anything that looks like a recovery phrase, private key or password is refused by the app and hidden again by our server before a chat is stored. Chats are deleted after 180 days.

Who sees it

The bWalletX team at The Bitcoin Corporation Ltd, to fix bugs and improve b. Your messages pass through Anthropic, who run the model. We never sell your chats.

Opting out

If you don't want your chats used to train anything, email support@bwalletx.com and we'll exclude them. To keep chats off our server entirely, add your own AI key in Settings › b agent: messages then go straight from your device to your provider.
